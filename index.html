Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Wanted to Ask (and a Bit More)

How do I book a shoot? +
Super simple — just message me through any convenient platform (Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, or the contact form on this site). We’ll discuss your preferred date, city, and session type. Once confirmed, I’ll reserve the date for you and send over preparation tips.
Can I choose the location myself? +
Absolutely! Suggest a place you love, or I’ll recommend beautiful spots with great light and atmosphere to match your vision and mood.
How long does the shoot take? +
It depends on the package: on average, sessions last 1–4 hours. We’ll plan the pace and type together — from relaxed city strolls to more dynamic/editorial sessions.
When will I receive the photos? +
Usually within 7–14 days (up to 30 for weddings). Delivery is via an online gallery with full-quality downloads.
Will you help me pose? +
Of course — I’ll guide you gently (where to look, how to move) so you feel comfortable and natural. No stiff or awkward posing.
What should I wear? +
I’ll send a style guide and help coordinate looks. The key is that you feel confident and the outfits fit the concept.
Do you retouch the photos? +
Yes. Clean, natural retouching — no fake plastic skin. Extra retouch is available on request.
Can I get the RAW files? +
I don’t provide RAWs. You’ll receive the best edited images, ready for printing and sharing.
